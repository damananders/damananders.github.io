Main project

AnyPS: going native

AnyPS started as one launcher for six generations of PlayStation emulators. Now I'm taking it further: rebuilding each console from scratch, from public hardware documentation, so games run straight inside AnyPS with no separate emulator behind it.

  • PS1Crash Team Racing is fully playable on my own core. A handful of other games boot, and I'm chasing the timing bugs that stop the rest.
  • PS2Boots the system menu and draws about 18 of the 27 games in my test library, including full 3D fights in Soulcalibur II and driving around in The Simpsons Hit & Run.
  • Up nextFixing the last per-game bugs on PS1 and PS2, then on to PSP, Vita, PS3 and PS4.

Long-term research, not a finished product. Bring-your-own games and system files, nothing redistributed. See AnyPS →

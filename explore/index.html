Explore

What you can do here

DMA is DaManAnders' corner of the internet, built around loving games old and new. You don't need an account to look around — signing up just unlocks the parts where you leave your own mark. Here's the whole place at a glance.

Rate & review games

Wiki-Chip Dex — the heart of the site. Over 336,000 games across ~75 platforms, from the Atari 2600 to PS5, PC and Switch.

  • Score any game 0–5 with chips, not stars — the “Chippers” (Poobar, Eskritt, Meepo, Gibby, Perisa, Fromie) in their real mood colours.
  • Write reviews, read everyone else's, and heart the games you love.
  • Build lists — backlog, favourites, top ten — and compare games side by side.
  • Talk in the forums, browse by platform, and check the leaderboard.
  • Follow people and watch a feed of what they're rating and saying.
  • Link your Steam or Battle.net library to your profile.

Your account & profile

One DMA Account works across the whole site, and it's yours to shape.

  • Sign up / sign in with a spam check and optional two-factor (2FA).
  • Pick a handle — it checks instantly whether it's free and it's yours the moment you save.
  • Customise your look: live-preview banners, frames and name styles, plus a motto, pronouns, status and bio.
  • Add gamer tags and links so people can find you.
  • Make buddies, send messages, follow people, and earn rewards that unlock extra cosmetics.
  • Your sign-in syncs across your devices. The profile chip is top-right on every page.

The projects

Two big homemade projects, each with its own page.

  • AnyPS — one launcher that plays across every PlayStation generation. Download it (full or lighter 32-bit build), see the screenshot gallery, roadmap and compatibility list.
  • AndersCraft — a private World of Warcraft server you can sign up for, with live realm status and a couple of realms (bonus-companions and hardcore).

Play & hang out

Quick fun and community, no download required.

  • Arcade — little browser games like Wizard of the Sprint and Supreme Lobbing (which hides a secret mode once you get good).
  • Stream — when Anders is live on Twitch, the page embeds the stream and a chat so you can watch without leaving.
  • Discord — join “Friends of DaManAnders”; live member and online counts show in the header too.

Make it yours

Open the settings — the gear at the top-left, next to the site menu button — and the site bends to how you like it.

  • Light or dark theme, or “Follow the Sun” so it tracks daylight where you are.
  • Seasonal skins (classic, fall, winter, spring, summer), each with its own gentle soundscape.
  • A local-time clock in the top-right, with a 12- or 24-hour switch.
  • Accessibility: bigger text, higher contrast, a reading font, and a “lite” mode for slow connections.
  • Reduce motion, toggle UI sounds, and navigate the whole site with a controller.
  • A floating site menu fans out every page; switch it between a playful floating layout and a tidy grid.

Little extras & learning more

The corners worth poking around.

  • Trophy Cabinet — earn trophies just by exploring and using the site; some are well hidden.
  • Hidden secrets — secret words and little easter eggs are tucked around for the curious to find.
  • Now / What's New — the landing page shows what Anders is building now; the Changelog lists site updates.
  • Projects, Uses & Résumé — the full list of what's being built, the gear behind it, and an interactive CV.
  • Links I Like — a hand-picked, community-ranked list of sites worth a look.
  • Support — chip in to help keep the projects online, if you'd like.

The best way in is still the front door — start at the search bar or the Wiki-Chip Dex banner and wander from there.

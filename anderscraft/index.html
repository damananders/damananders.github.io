    Questions

    FAQ

    How do I get the client?

    You need the AndersCraft game client, and it is handed out in Discord. Ask there. The custom races and items need that client's patch files.

    Which realm should I pick?

    The main realm has player bots that keep the world busy and can fill out your group, with rates around 2x. Andersian Hardcore gives you one life and has no bots. If you are not sure, start on the main realm.

    Can I play alone?

    Yes. On the main realm bots can fill out your group, and dungeons scale to your group size, so it works solo.

    What does hardcore mean here?

    Andersian Hardcore is the second realm. You get one life, and there are no bots: you walk alone as one of Azeroth's Chosen.

    The sign-up says it is offline. What now?

    The sign-up service runs on the server PC, so sign-ups only work while it is on. Try again later, or check Discord.

    I forgot my password.

    There is no password reset on this site. Ask in Discord.

    What makes this different from a stock server?

    It is WotLK 3.3.5a (build 12340) on an AzerothCore-based server, with a lot changed: a level cap of 125, any race as any class including Goblins and Worgen, any gear once you buy the proficiency, old-world flying, transmog, account-wide mounts, dungeons that scale to your group, cross-faction battlegrounds, a bigger bank, Mythic+ dungeons and an auction house kept stocked by a bot.

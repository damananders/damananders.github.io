The Great Andersian Update

A new currency, new faces in the capitals, things roaming the wilds, rewards for the brave, and a road past level 125. Pick a chapter.

AndersCraft has its own coin now. Earn it by playing, spend it on things gold can't buy.

  • Andersian MarkThe new server currency. You earn it at level milestones, from roaming rares, from the Bounty Board and from hidden caches.Live
  • The Andersian ExchangeMarrek, Keeper of the Exchange, trades your Marks for the Regalia of the Andersian (30 Marks a piece) and Heartflame (50 Marks). Find him at the Stormwind and Orgrimmar banks.Live
  • Regalia of the AndersianThe server's signature transmog set: eight pieces of crimson, epic quality.Live
  • Heartflame, the Maker's RelicA relic any class can equip, with Stamina and critical strike.Live

Four new residents at the Stormwind and Orgrimmar banks.

  • Vaultkeeper ThessalyA banker with extra storage, for when the regular vault is full of things you will definitely need one day.Live
  • Mirra, the ShapewrightA barber for hair, face and skin.Live
  • Marrek, Keeper of the ExchangeTrades Andersian Marks for the Regalia and Heartflame.Live
  • The FallenA memorial that names every fallen Chosen of the hardcore realm, newest first: their name, their level, and what killed them.Live

Things to hunt, hand in and dig up, all paying out in Marks.

  • Roaming raresOld Scorchmaw, the Tallgrass Phantom and Gnashclaw the Patient wander Kalimdor. They scale to the zone, and each drops 3 to 6 Marks.Live
  • The Bounty BoardA new bounty every day: bring the day's item for Marks. In Stormwind and Orgrimmar.Live
  • Hidden Andersian CachesChests tucked away in the open world. Each holds 5 to 10 Marks, with a chance at a Regalia piece or Heartflame.Live
  • The Fallen memorialEvery hardcore death is remembered, whether it was a dragon or a very determined boar.Live

On Andersian Hardcore, type .chosen to take the oath. Survive, and the world takes notice.

  1. 60the ResoluteTitle and the Sigil of the Lone Path tabard
  2. 80the UnyieldingTitle and the Crest of the Unyielding tabard
  3. 125the EternalTitle, the Aegis of the Alone tabard, and an eternal aura that glows around you
  • The VoiceA narrator whispers to you at levels 10, 50, 80 and 125, on every realm.Live

    Questions

    FAQ

    How do I get the client?

    You need the AndersCraft game client, and it is handed out in Discord. Ask there. The custom races and items need that client's patch files.

    Which realm should I pick?

    The main realm has player bots that keep the world busy and can fill out your group, with rates around 2x. Andersian Hardcore gives you one life and has no bots. If you are not sure, start on the main realm.

    Can I play alone?

    Yes. On the main realm bots can fill out your group, and dungeons scale to your group size, so it works solo.

    What does hardcore mean here?

    Andersian Hardcore is the second realm. You get one life, and there are no bots: you walk alone as one of Azeroth's Chosen.

    What are Andersian Marks?

    The server's own currency. You earn Marks at level milestones, from the roaming rares on Kalimdor, from the daily Bounty Board and from hidden caches. Spend them with Marrek at the Stormwind or Orgrimmar bank on the Regalia of the Andersian transmog set or Heartflame, a relic any class can use.

    What is Azeroth's Chosen?

    An oath you can take on Andersian Hardcore by typing .chosen. Chosen earn a title and tabard at levels 60, 80 and 125 (the Resolute, the Unyielding, the Eternal), and an eternal aura at 125. If you fall, the Fallen memorial remembers you.

    What happens after level 125?

    Your experience keeps going into Titan Echoes, prestige levels with no cap. Earning them is live now. The aura that gives +4 to all stats per Echo is coming soon.

    The sign-up says it is offline. What now?

    The sign-up service runs on the server PC, so sign-ups only work while it is on. Try again later, or check Discord.

    I forgot my password.

    There is no password reset on this site. Ask in Discord.

    What makes this different from a stock server?

    It is WotLK 3.3.5a (build 12340) on an AzerothCore-based server, with a lot changed: a level cap of 125, any race as any class including Goblins and Worgen, any gear once you buy the proficiency, old-world flying, transmog, account-wide mounts, dungeons that scale to your group, cross-faction battlegrounds, a bigger bank, Mythic+ dungeons, an auction house kept stocked by a bot, its own currency (Andersian Marks) with world events to earn it, and Titan Echoes past level 125.
